About Us

Investment Philosophy

Grounded in fundamentals. Focused on long term value creation through strategic multi-family acquisitions.

Lynn Investments Ltd is a private real estate investment firm specializing in multi-family residential properties. We believe that well located, professionally managed apartment buildings represent one of the most resilient and rewarding asset classes in real estate.

Our approach combines rigorous underwriting with active asset management to unlock value at every stage of the investment lifecycle. From acquisition through stabilization and long term hold, we focus on properties where operational improvements and strategic repositioning can drive meaningful returns.

We partner with investors who share our long term perspective and commitment to building portfolios of enduring quality.

Disciplined Underwriting

Every acquisition is evaluated through a rigorous analytical framework focused on fundamental value drivers and downside protection.

Active Management

We take a hands on approach to property operations, identifying opportunities to improve performance and enhance asset value from day one.

Long Term Orientation

Our investment horizon allows us to make decisions that compound value over time rather than chase short term gains.

Meet Our CEO

EE

Evan Ekstein

Chief Executive Officer

Evan Ekstein founded Lynn Investments Ltd with a clear vision: to build a portfolio of high quality multi-family properties that generate consistent, long term value for investors. His approach combines institutional rigor with entrepreneurial agility, allowing the firm to identify and capitalize on opportunities that larger players often overlook.

With a Master's degree in Real Estate Development from Nova Southeastern University, Evan brings deep academic grounding and practical expertise to every investment decision. His education provided a comprehensive foundation in development finance, market analysis, and the strategic frameworks that guide the firm's investment thesis.

As a licensed Royal LePage real estate agent in Toronto, Evan maintains direct market insight and relationships across the Greater Toronto Area's dynamic real estate landscape. This on the ground presence informs the firm's acquisition strategy and provides a competitive edge in sourcing off market opportunities.

Beyond investing, Evan advises real estate developers on profit optimization and expansion strategy, helping them scale their businesses and maximize returns across their development pipelines.

Our Process

Investment Approach

A methodical, four stage process that guides every investment from initial evaluation through long term value realization.

01

Identify

We continuously monitor target markets for multi-family properties that meet our investment criteria, leveraging proprietary deal flow and established broker relationships.

02

Evaluate

Each opportunity undergoes comprehensive financial analysis, physical inspection, and market assessment to validate the investment thesis and quantify risk adjusted returns.

03

Execute

Upon acquisition, we implement a tailored business plan focused on operational improvements, capital enhancements, and revenue optimization to unlock embedded value.

04

Optimize

Ongoing active management ensures each asset performs at its highest potential, with regular performance reviews and strategic adjustments to maximize long term returns.
